01 / UNDERSTAND THE ISSUE

What are we
looking at?

Not everything online is what it appears to be. Understanding the terms is the first step towards safer digital habits.

✳

AI deepfake

Synthetic or manipulated audio, video or images made or altered using artificial intelligence to make someone appear to say or do something they did not.

◉

02 / SPOT THE WARNING SIGNS

Look closer.
Question more.

These clues can help you decide when to investigate further. No single visual clue can reliably prove that something is fake.

01

Unnatural details

Watch for odd reflections, inconsistent lighting, strange edges, or details that change between frames.

02

Audio and lip mismatch

Listen for unnatural pauses, unusual tone, or speech that does not seem to match mouth movement.

03

Emotional pressure

Be cautious when a post uses panic, outrage, urgency or shocking claims to make you react quickly.

04

Missing context

Look for absent dates, locations, original sources or a longer version that could change the meaning.

05

Untrusted account

Check the account history, website address, author details and whether reputable outlets confirm the claim.

06

Unusual requests

Verify unexpected requests for money, passwords, OTPs or urgent action through another trusted channel.

ⓘ

Important: AI detection tools can make mistakes. Treat their results as clues, not final proof. Verify the original source and context.

03 / YOUR 5-STEP VERIFICATION ROUTINE

Before you share,
run the checks.

Use this simple checklist whenever a post, image, audio clip or video seems surprising or suspicious.

1

Stop and slow down

Do not forward immediately. Notice whether the post is trying to trigger a strong reaction.

2

Find the original source

Who first published it? Check the account, website, date and original upload.

3

Cross-check the claim

Search for independent confirmation from reliable news outlets or official sources.

4

Inspect the context

Use reverse image search where appropriate. Check location, date and the full clip—not just a crop.

5

Decide responsibly

If you cannot verify it, do not share it as fact. Correct mistakes respectfully and report harmful content.
